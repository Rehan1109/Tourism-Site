Madhya Pradesh

Explore the soul of India.

Discover ancient temples, rich forests, sacred sites, and beautiful hill cities in one amazing state.

Travel note

Four memorable places.

Best time to visit: October to March for comfortable sightseeing and heritage travel.

Heritage

Khajuraho

Famous for its carved temples and artistic stone work that reflect India's rich history.

More info

Khajuraho is known for its beautiful temple architecture and ancient sculptures.

Wildlife

Kanha

Known for tiger safaris, green forests, and a peaceful natural environment.

More info

Kanha is one of the best places in India for jungle safaris and wildlife spotting.

Spiritual

Sanchi

The Great Stupa is an important Buddhist monument with calm surroundings and deep history.

More info

Sanchi is famous for its ancient Buddhist Stupa and beautiful carved gateways.

Romance

Mandu

A beautiful historical city with forts, palaces, scenic views, and a romantic feel.

More info

Mandu is known for its hilltop views, royal ruins, and beautiful historic architecture.
